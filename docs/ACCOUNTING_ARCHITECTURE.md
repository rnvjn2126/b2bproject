# Accounting architecture

A separate Django app references the existing company workspace. Account codes, group codes and posting keys are workspace scoped. An Indian starter chart is installed on demand and can be extended. The first UI uses an April–March financial year with monthly periods. The posting interface uses accounting dates in India time. Dimensions are workspace-specific branches and cost centres.

Money is accepted as Decimal with at most two fractional digits. Each line is debit or credit, every posted journal has at least two lines and its debit/credit totals agree. A transaction posts the header and lines together. Posting and period lock operations use the same workspace row lock on PostgreSQL. The unique submission key prevents duplicate posting, and replaying the same key with different data is rejected.

Opening balances use one balanced opening journal per workspace. Period reports derive opening, movements and closing amounts from posted lines. Adjustments are separate journals. Corrections reverse the original lines in a new journal in an open period. Only one reversal of an original is allowed. The initial scope uses INR and a single branch and cost centre per journal. Branch-by-branch opening imports and year-end retained earnings carry-forward remain separate work. Period locks are one-way in the initial UI.

Receipt and payment vouchers move an explicitly selected amount into or out of the starter cash or bank ledgers. Contra vouchers transfer between those two ledgers. Voucher types are validated in the posting service as well as the form. A payment reference is descriptive. These vouchers do not clear invoices or verify a bank transaction.

Financial statements use posted lines. Profit and loss reports activity within the selected date range. The balance sheet includes all entries through the end date, plus accumulated net activity still recorded in income and expense accounts. Branch filters include only journals explicitly assigned to that branch. These are ledger summaries. Inventory costing, gross and operating profit classifications, period closing and statutory presentation remain separate work.

For the rest of Phase 2, keep an accepted quotation and its downstream order separate from financial posting. Issue a document and its journal within one database transaction, with a unique link from the issued document to its posting and immutable copies of prices, items and party details. Allocate quantities to deliveries and invoices under document locks so replays and concurrent requests cannot bill the same quantity twice. The statutory invoice design and the workflow for confirming the posting event still need review.
